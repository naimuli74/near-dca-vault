How it works

Three steps. Dry-run stops after the quote. Live mode is something you opt into, per interval.

1

Create a plan

Choose USDC on a supported chain, a target asset, a destination address, a size, a slippage limit and a schedule.

2

Get a real quote

Each interval asks 1Click for a quote. Dry-run stops there. If the price is worse than your limit after fees, the buy is skipped.

3

You send, if you want

In live mode you fund that interval's deposit address yourself. The asset lands in your own address. We never hold the funds.

FAQ

Is it custodial?

No. This app never holds your funds or your keys. Dry-run does not issue a deposit address. In live mode you send each interval yourself to a fresh 1Click address, and the bought asset is delivered to an address you control.

What if a swap fails?

1Click can refund to the refund address you set. History shows FAILED or REFUNDED, with explorer links when 1Click provides them. The app cannot move funds. Check the refund address and the NEAR Intents explorer.

Why is dry-run the default?

There is no Intents testnet. Dry-run still asks 1Click for a real quote with dry: true, so you can see fees and the slippage gate without sending funds. Live mode is opt-in and mainnet-only.

Why isn't there a standing vault?

1Click deposit addresses are unique and expire in about 10 minutes, so a recurring vault cannot sit on one address. A later version could use a NEAR contract and Chain Signatures. That is not in this build.

Are the quotes on this page real?

Yes. The ticker, the preview, and dry-run history all come from unauthenticated 1Click dry quotes. The simulator is different: it uses CoinGecko historical USD prices only, and says so next to the result.
